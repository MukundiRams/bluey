# Standard Bank South Africa — Credit Product Catalogue

**Snapshot:** 14 September 2026

## Personal lending

Current product families:
- Term Loan
- Revolving Loan
- Overdraft
- Loan Consolidation
- Energy Loan
- Student Loans
- Investment Backed Lending
- Budget Flex

### Term Loan — current advertised facts

- Minimum monthly income: R3,000
- Maximum loan amount: R300,000
- Term: 12–84 months
- Personalised interest rate
- Fixed monthly repayments
- Unsecured
- Credit life insurance included
- Monthly service fee: R69
- No early termination fee stated on the current product page

The current page gives an illustrative example and explicitly states it is not a contract.

Source: https://www.standardbank.co.za/southafrica/personal/products-and-services/borrow-for-your-needs/personal-loans/our-loans/term-loan

### Energy Loan
Current catalogue states:
- minimum monthly income R8,000
- maximum loan R300,000
- term 12–72 months
- personalised rate

### Investment Backed Lending
Current catalogue states:
- minimum R50,000
- maximum 50% of eligible portfolio value
- conditions apply

### Budget Flex
Current catalogue describes this as an interest-free short-term structured repayment facility for eligible recent card purchases, with a displayed maximum of R10,000.
