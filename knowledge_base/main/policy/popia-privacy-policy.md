# Standard Bank — POPIA and customer privacy

## What POPIA is
The Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) governs how South African organisations, including Standard Bank, may collect, use, store, and share customers' personal information.

## What information Standard Bank collects
- Identity and contact details (name, ID number, address, phone, email).
- Financial information (account balances, transaction history, income, credit profile).
- Information required for FICA compliance (see "fica-kyc-policy").
- Interaction data, including chat conversations with Bluey, for service improvement and record-keeping.

## Why Standard Bank collects it
- To open and administer accounts, process transactions, and meet regulatory obligations (FICA, tax reporting, exchange control).
- To assess creditworthiness for credit products.
- To detect and prevent fraud.
- To improve products and customer service, including training and improving AI assistants like Bluey — always in de-identified or aggregated form where used for this purpose.

## Sharing information
- Standard Bank does not sell customer personal information.
- Information may be shared with: credit bureaus (for credit applications), the South African Revenue Service and SARB (regulatory reporting), law enforcement (where legally compelled), and service providers who process data on Standard Bank's behalf under contractual confidentiality obligations.

## Customer rights under POPIA
- **Access**: customers may request a copy of the personal information Standard Bank holds about them.
- **Correction**: customers may request correction of inaccurate information.
- **Objection**: customers may object to processing for direct marketing at any time.
- **Deletion**: customers may request deletion of information no longer required for a lawful purpose — note that regulatory retention periods (e.g. FICA records must be kept for 5 years after the account relationship ends) override deletion requests during that period.

## How to exercise these rights
Requests can be submitted via the app (Settings > Privacy > Data request), in-branch, or by asking Bluey to route the request to a human banker — Bluey itself cannot action a formal POPIA data request, only escalate it.

## Data security
Customer data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and access is restricted to staff and systems with a legitimate business need, logged and audited in line with Standard Bank's information security policy.
