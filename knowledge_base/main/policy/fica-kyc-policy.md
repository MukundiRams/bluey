# Standard Bank — FICA and KYC requirements

## Why we ask
The Financial Intelligence Centre Act (FICA) requires all South African banks to verify a customer's identity, address, and (where relevant) source of funds before opening an account or processing certain transactions. This protects customers and the financial system from fraud, money laundering, and terrorist financing.

## Documents required to open an account
- **Proof of identity**: South African green bar-coded ID book, smart ID card, or valid passport (for foreign nationals, also a valid visa/permit).
- **Proof of residential address**: a document not older than 3 months — utility bill, bank statement from another institution, municipal rates account, or a signed lease agreement.
- **Proof of income** (for credit products — overdrafts, credit cards, loans): latest 3 months' payslips, or 3 months' bank statements for self-employed applicants.
- **Proof of income source** (for large or unusual deposits): may be requested on a risk basis, e.g. sale agreement, inheritance letter, business invoice.

## Verification process
- Identity documents are verified electronically against Home Affairs records where possible; this is usually instant.
- Address verification may take up to 2 business days if it cannot be matched electronically.
- Business and trust accounts require additional documentation: company registration, trust deed, and identification of all directors/trustees/beneficial owners holding more than 25%.

## Ongoing due diligence
- Existing customers may occasionally be asked to re-verify or update documents (a "FICA refresh"), particularly if account activity changes significantly or documents have expired.
- Failure to complete a requested FICA refresh within 30 days may result in account restrictions (limited to receiving funds only) until resolved.

## Politically exposed persons (PEPs)
Customers who are, or are related to, a domestic or foreign politically exposed person must disclose this status. Enhanced due diligence applies and may extend account opening timelines.
