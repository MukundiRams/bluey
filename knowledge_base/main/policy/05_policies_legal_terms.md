## Regulatory

Standard Bank South Africa's regulatory area includes FAIS, USA Patriot Act documentation, Wolfsberg questionnaires and the complaints process.

The site states that Standard Bank is a licensed financial services provider under FAIS and a registered credit provider under the National Credit Act, registration number **NCRCP15**.

Source: https://www.standardbank.co.za/southafrica/personal/about-us/regulatory

## Code of Banking Practice

Standard Bank publishes a voluntary Code of Banking Practice aligned with the Conduct Standard for Banks and Treating Customers Fairly outcomes. It sets minimum customer-facing standards and describes customer and bank responsibilities within its stated scope.

Source: https://www.standardbank.co.za/static_file/South%20Africa/PDF/Legal%20and%20Regulatory/Code%20of%20Banking%20Practice_Digital_2025.pdf

## Electronic Instruction Indemnity

Standard Bank publishes an Electronic Instruction Indemnity for authority to accept and act on digital instructions that would ordinarily be accepted in original written form.

Source: https://www.standardbank.co.za/southafrica/personal/about-us/legal
