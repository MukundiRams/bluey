# Standard Bank Product Catalogue

Reference descriptions for the products used in `retailcore-pipeline-cleaned.ipynb`.

**Research note:** Descriptions are concise, plain-language summaries based on Standard Bank South Africa's public product categories and indexed product pages, checked on 2026-09-04. Product names, eligibility rules, pricing and availability can change. Some names in the notebook are modelling labels or broader product categories; they should be revalidated with Standard Bank before production use.

## Accounts and banking tiers

| Product | Description |
|---|---|
| MyMo Account | A low-cost everyday bank account for routine banking, card payments, digital banking and money transfers. Standard Bank's indexed page describes it as available without a monthly income requirement. |
| Achieva Account | An everyday transactional account aimed at customers earning at least the stated minimum gross monthly income. It provides day-to-day banking and access to Standard Bank digital channels. |
| Prestige Banking | A premium personal banking tier for established earners, combining a transactional account with enhanced service, benefits and relationship support. The indexed Standard Bank result lists a minimum monthly income of R25,000. |
| Private Banking | A high-value banking service for affluent customers, generally combining tailored relationship management, specialist advice, wealth planning and premium banking benefits. |
| Signature Banking | An upper-tier banking proposition for very high-income customers, with a premium relationship service and access to specialised banking and lifestyle benefits. |

## Credit cards

| Product | Description |
|---|---|
| Blue Credit Card | An entry-level credit card for everyday purchases, with a revolving credit facility subject to affordability and credit assessment. |
| Gold Credit Card | A mainstream credit card offering a higher tier of everyday credit and card benefits than an entry-level card, subject to affordability and credit assessment. |
| Titanium Credit Card | A higher-tier credit card for customers seeking increased credit access and additional benefits, subject to affordability and credit assessment. |
| Platinum Credit Card | A premium credit card with enhanced benefits and services for customers meeting the relevant income and credit criteria. |
| World Citizen Credit Card | A premium travel-oriented credit card designed for customers who want international payment capability and travel-related benefits, subject to the bank's criteria. |
| Diners Club Platinum Credit Card | A premium Diners Club card offering credit facilities and travel and lifestyle benefits, including access to participating airport lounge services where the applicable terms allow. |

## Lending and credit facilities

| Product | Description |
|---|---|
| Term Loan | A fixed-term personal loan where an approved amount is repaid through scheduled instalments over an agreed period, with interest and fees determined by the customer's assessment. |
| Revolving Loan | A reusable credit facility that allows an approved customer to draw, repay and draw again up to an agreed limit. Interest is charged on the amount used, subject to the facility terms. |
| Overdraft | A linked transactional-account credit facility that allows eligible customers to spend beyond their available balance up to an approved limit. It is intended for short-term cash-flow needs and attracts interest and applicable fees. |
| Investment Backed Lending | A lending facility secured by qualifying investments or assets. It can provide liquidity without requiring the customer to sell the underlying investment, subject to collateral, margin and credit requirements. |
| Vehicle and Asset Finance | Finance used to purchase a vehicle or other qualifying asset, normally repaid in instalments over an agreed term while the asset provides security for the facility. |
| Home Loan | A mortgage facility used to buy, build or refinance residential property. Repayment is made over an agreed term and the property is generally used as security. |
| Student Loan | Education finance intended to cover qualifying study-related costs such as tuition and, where approved, other education expenses. Eligibility, repayment and security requirements depend on the product terms and applicant assessment. |

## Savings and investments

| Product | Description |
|---|---|
| Savings Account | An interest-bearing deposit account for building or holding savings while retaining access to funds according to the account rules. |
| Fixed Deposit | A savings or investment account in which money is placed for a chosen fixed term at an agreed interest rate. Access before maturity may be restricted or subject to conditions. |
| Tax-Free Savings Account | A long-term savings or investment account designed to provide tax-free returns within the annual and lifetime contribution limits set by South African tax legislation. |

## Self-service how-to (account actions)

These entries cover common self-service account actions. Each provides the official Standard Bank link a verified customer can use to complete the action. **These actions concern a customer's own account, so identity must be verified (via SA ID) before the link is shared.**

| Action | How-to and link |
|---|---|
| View or change your card PIN | You can view or change your PIN in the Standard Bank Mobile App or Internet Banking. Manage your PIN here: https://www.standardbank.co.za/southafrica/personal/products/bank-cards/manage-your-card |
| Reset your Internet Banking / app password | Reset your password from the login screen of the app or Internet Banking, or manage your profile here: https://www.standardbank.co.za/southafrica/personal/products/ways-to-bank/digital-banking |
| View or download account statements | Verified customers can view and download statements in the app or Internet Banking. Learn more here: https://www.standardbank.co.za/southafrica/personal/products/ways-to-bank/digital-banking |
| Report a lost or stolen card / block a card | Block a lost or stolen card immediately in the app, or call the 24/7 line. Card safety and blocking: https://www.standardbank.co.za/southafrica/personal/products/bank-cards/manage-your-card |
| Order or replace a bank card | Order or replace a card through the app, Internet Banking, or a branch. Card management: https://www.standardbank.co.za/southafrica/personal/products/bank-cards/manage-your-card |
| Update your contact details | Update your cellphone number, email, or address in the app or Internet Banking under your profile: https://www.standardbank.co.za/southafrica/personal/products/ways-to-bank/digital-banking |

**Research note:** These links point to Standard Bank South Africa's public product and digital-banking pages, and are provided so a verified customer can complete the action on the official site. Exact deep links and page structure can change; revalidate against Standard Bank before production use.
