# Standard Bank South Africa — Product Catalogue Knowledge Base

**Scope:** Personal and business banking products offered by The Standard Bank of South Africa Limited (Reg. No. 1962/000738/06), an authorised financial services provider (FSP 11287) and registered credit provider (NCRCP15).
**Pricing basis:** 2026 published pricing guides and product pages. Fees are reviewed annually (typically each January) and are subject to change at the bank's discretion — always confirm against the current official pricing guide before quoting a customer.
**Last compiled:** 15 September 2026

## 2. Credit Cards

All cards are subject to a National Credit Act (NCA) affordability assessment; approved credit limits and interest rates are personalised. Applicants must be 18+, SA resident with a valid ID, or hold a valid passport and work permit.

| Card | Monthly fee | Minimum monthly income | Credit limit | Notable benefits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Blue | ~R40 | R5,000 | Up to R250,000 | Entry-level, UCount-eligible, 55-day interest-free period |
| Gold | ~R63–R64 | R5,000 | Up to R250,000 | Mastercard Priceless Specials, UCount Rewards |
| Titanium | ~R89 | R25,000 | Up to R250,000 | Lounge visits, Emirates flight discounts, travel insurance |
| Platinum | ~R108 | R58,000 | Up to R250,000 | Legacy Silver membership, lounge access |
| World Citizen | ~R209 | R92,000 | Up to R250,000 | Premium travel rewards, multiple lounge visits |
| Diners Club Platinum | ~R128 | R500,000 | Personalised | Global lounge access, 24/7 concierge, travel insurance |
| Diners Club Shari'ah Charge Card | ~R128 | Varies | Personalised | Shari'ah-compliant, no interest charged |
| Business Credit Card | ~R45 | Business-qualifying | Personalised | 55-day interest-free period for business spend |

**Shared terms across the range:**
- Once-off initiation fee: ~R190
- Interest-free period: up to 55 days on purchases
- Minimum monthly repayment: 3% of outstanding balance
- Personalised interest rate range: roughly prime − 0.25% up to the NCA-capped maximum (currently 21% ceiling under the in-duplum/NCA rules)
- UCount Rewards: optional, roughly R20/month add-on
- Secondary/supplementary card: additional monthly fee (~R26)

## 7. General Notes for Internal Use

- **Fee tiers change annually** — Standard Bank republishes personal and business pricing guides each year (usually effective from January), so figures above should be revalidated each pricing cycle rather than hard-coded.
- **NCA caps** apply across all credit products (credit cards, personal loans, vehicle finance): maximum interest rate is prime plus a regulated margin, and affordability assessments are mandatory regardless of product tier.
- **UCount Rewards** is a common thread across transactional accounts and credit cards — it's an optional paid add-on, not bundled free, and its value depends on redemption behaviour.
- **PayShap** is increasingly the low-cost instant payment rail (flat ~R2 fee) compared with traditional immediate payments, which scale with transaction value.
- Real-world fee amounts above are drawn from published 2026 pricing guides and product pages; some figures (e.g. home loan initiation fees) are indexed annually and should be treated as indicative bands rather than exact current-day values.
