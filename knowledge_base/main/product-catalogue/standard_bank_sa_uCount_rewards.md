# Standard Bank South Africa — UCount Rewards Programme

**Category:** Loyalty / Rewards Programme  
**Jurisdiction:** South Africa only  
**Snapshot date:** 15 September 2026  
**Source policy:** Standard Bank South Africa / UCount Rewards official websites and official programme documents only.

> **Important:** UCount publishes programme rules, terms and conditions, retailer lists, earn rates and promotions that can change. The public UCount site currently shows an **Earn Rate Changes** notice effective **16 June 2026**, while the latest detailed programme-rules document located for this corpus is dated **July 2025**. Where the current public site and an older detailed rule differ, the current website notice and the latest applicable programme rules should take precedence.

## 1. What is UCount Rewards?

UCount Rewards is Standard Bank South Africa's loyalty programme. Members earn Rewards Points when they use qualifying Standard Bank cards and products/services in ways defined by the programme rules.

The current UCount website summarises the proposition as shop, earn, redeem and repeat.

The programme uses **Rewards Points** for the rewards currency and **Tiering Points** to determine a member's Tier Level.

Official source:
https://ucount.standardbank.co.za/

## 2. How the programme works

A customer's UCount experience has two separate concepts.

### Rewards Points

Rewards Points have a Rand value and can be used for eligible redemptions.

The latest detailed programme rules located for this corpus list six reward categories:

1. Card Rewards
2. Choose Your Own Rewards (CYOR)
3. Fuel Rewards
4. Retailer Rewards
5. Standard Bank Mobile Data Rewards
6. Goals and Gains

### Tiering Points

Tiering Points measure qualifying banking activity and determine the customer's UCount Tier Level.

The detailed July 2025 programme rules define:

| Tier | Tiering Points |
|---|---:|
| Tier 1 | 0–399 |
| Tier 2 | 400–574 |
| Tier 3 | 575–724 |
| Tier 4 | 725–874 |
| Tier 5 | 875+ |

The current UCount website also confirms that Tiering Points are based on banking activity and the products used.

## 5. Rewards earning

### 5.1 Card Rewards

The detailed July 2025 programme rules state that qualifying card purchases can earn Rewards Points at rates determined by:

- Tier Level; and
- card type.

The July 2025 table states:

| Card | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Tier 3 | Tier 4 | Tier 5 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Credit Card | 0.25% | 0.40% | 0.50% | 0.70% | 1.00% |
| Debit/Cheque Card | 0.05% | 0.10% | 0.12% | 0.15% | 0.25% |

The detailed rules specify a **R50,000 spend cap per fixed cycle** for the table above.

The rules also state that Evolve card holders automatically earn at credit-card rates for Card Rewards.

#### Important exclusions / exceptions

The detailed rules state that:

- Card Rewards are not earned on qualifying CYOR purchases in the chosen grocery, fashion or lifestyle category;
- fuel purchases at participating fuel retailers do not earn Card Rewards because they are handled under Fuel Rewards;
- transactions using SnapScan, Masterpass or Zapper have a maximum of 5,000 Rewards Points under the relevant Card Rewards rule;
- no Card Rewards are earned on SnapScan wallet transactions;
- qualifying Payflex transactions earn Card Rewards only.

## 6. Choose Your Own Rewards (CYOR)

Choose Your Own Rewards lets the customer choose a category:

- Grocery
- Fashion
- Lifestyle

The detailed programme rules state that the applicable earn rate depends on:

- Tier Level;
- how much the customer spends; and
- whether the card is a qualifying Credit, Debit or Cheque Card.

The detailed July 2025 rules state the following base CYOR rates:

| Card | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Tier 3 | Tier 4 | Tier 5 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Credit Card | 2% | 3% | 4% | 10% | 20% |
| Debit/Cheque Card | 0.5% | 1% | 1.5% | 2.5% | 5% |

The standard CYOR spend cap is described as the lower of:

- 20% of total spend on the card; or
- R3,000 on the chosen CYOR category.

## 9. Standard Bank Mobile Data Rewards

The programme rules include a Standard Bank Mobile Data Rewards category.

The detailed rules state that qualifying customers can receive data rewards subject to specific account/product and monthly airtime/data requirements.

One important condition in the 2025 rules is that certain qualifying Standard Bank accounts must purchase at least **R10 airtime or data in a month** to receive the applicable data reward.

Because mobile products and UCount rules can change, verify current eligibility in the latest programme rules.

## 10. Goals & Gains

Goals & Gains is the UCount Rewards in-app platform.

Customers can receive personalised goals and, after completing qualifying activities, may receive a gain such as:

- Rewards Points;
- vouchers;
- coupons.

The July 2025 programme rules describe:

- goals as personalised activities;
- coupons as reward discounts;
- vouchers as cash-value codes.

The rules state that after completing an activity, the customer has **two weeks** to choose the available gain. Rewards Points are allocated immediately when that option is selected; vouchers/coupons are valid for two weeks from selection unless otherwise stated.

Standard Bank's current Banking App UCount add-on also exposes Goals & Gains and lets members see Current, Achieved, Pending and Expired goals.

## 11. Tiering — how customers move up

The UCount Tier Level is based on monthly banking activity and qualifying Standard Bank products/services.

The detailed July 2025 rules show qualifying activities across areas including:

- digital banking activity;
- subscription payments;
- Goals & Gains;
- active Standard Bank Mobile / Connect SIMs;
- marketing consent;
- savings and investments;
- transactional account balances;
- debit orders and monthly deposits;
- using qualifying credit-card spend versus debit/cheque spend;

- vehicle and asset finance;
- revolving loans;
- personal/term/solar/medium-term loans;
- overdrafts;
- qualifying insurance;
- wills;

## 17. Security and fraud warnings

Standard Bank's UCount site currently warns about fraudsters impersonating UCount Rewards using phishing emails and smishing SMS messages linked to Rewards Points redemptions.

Standard Bank specifically warns customers:

- do not click links in suspicious UCount messages;
- do not open unexpected attachments;
- do not provide an OTP;
- do not scan QR codes from suspicious emails/SMS messages to redeem Rewards Points;
- report suspected fraud to **0800 222 050**.

## 19. FAQ

### What is UCount Rewards?
Standard Bank South Africa's loyalty programme that rewards qualifying banking and card activity.

### What are Rewards Points?
Points earned through qualifying UCount activities that can be redeemed for eligible goods, services, benefits or qualifying Standard Bank products.

### What are Tiering Points?
Points based on monthly qualifying banking activity. They determine the customer's UCount Tier Level.

### Does a higher Tier give more Rewards Points?
Generally yes. The programme rules tie many earn rates to Tier Level, and the current UCount site describes higher tiers as enabling faster rewards and additional benefits.

### What are the UCount Tier Levels?
Tier 1 through Tier 5, with the detailed programme rules defining Tier 1 as 0–399 points and Tier 5 as 875+.

### What is Choose Your Own Rewards?
A facility allowing the member to select Grocery, Fashion or Lifestyle as a preferred rewards category.

### How much can I earn on fuel?
The current live UCount site advertises up to R10 per litre at participating Astron Energy service stations. Exact earning depends on the current rules, card, tier and any applicable promotion.

### Can I use Rewards Points to pay a Standard Bank loan?
Standard Bank currently promotes using Rewards Points to reduce a Revolving Loan or Term Loan balance, subject to qualifying product and redemption rules.

### Can I use Rewards Points for savings/investments?
The detailed programme rules permit redemption into qualifying products including PureSave, Notice Deposit, Tax-Free Investment Account and AutoShare Invest.

### Can I redeem points for vouchers?
Yes. The UCount site and programme rules support voucher redemptions through qualifying channels/retailers.

### Can I use Rewards Points for fuel?
Yes, subject to the current participating fuel-retailer and redemption rules.

### How do I see my UCount balance?
The Standard Bank Banking App's UCount add-on shows Rewards Points, Rand value and Tier Level.

### How do I improve my Tier?
Use the Tier Estimator and review qualifying activities such as digital banking activity, balances, products, lending, insurance and other activities included in the current programme rules.

### Does every Standard Bank transaction earn Rewards Points?
No. The programme has qualifying-purchase rules, exclusions, caps and card/product-specific conditions.

### Can I earn Rewards Points through SnapScan, Masterpass or Zapper?
There are special restrictions. The detailed rules cap certain Card Rewards at 5,000 points and state that SnapScan wallet transactions do not earn Card Rewards; CYOR has additional exclusions.

### Is there a UCount membership fee?
The detailed 2025 rules specify R25/month, with certain legacy/product exceptions. Current pricing should be checked before answering definitively.

## 20. Knowledge-base answer policy

When responding to a UCount customer question:

1. Identify whether the customer is asking about earning, tiering, redemption, fees, membership, retailers, or security.
2. Check the current UCount website for live rates and notices.
3. Use the latest programme rules for detailed eligibility, exclusions, caps and tier mechanics.
4. Treat promotional earn rates separately from standard programme rates.
5. Never assume a retailer remains participating solely because it appears in an older programme-rules PDF.
6. Never disclose or request a customer's UCount login credentials, OTP or PIN.
7. For fraud concerns, prioritise the official Standard Bank/UCount security instructions.
8. For Wealth and Investment clients, check their relationship-manager-specific rules because the programme documents state that some rules differ for those clients.
