# Standard Bank South Africa — Guides, Calculators & Financial Education

**Snapshot:** 14 September 2026

## Personal loan education

Standard Bank publishes guidance on personal-loan choice, affordability, repayment terms, credit scores, term vs revolving borrowing and preparing before applying.

Source: https://www.standardbank.co.za/southafrica/personal/learn/the-ins-and-outs-of-personal-loans

## Personal loan calculator

Current calculator functions include:
- borrowing estimate;
- monthly repayment estimate;
- first repayment date;
- monthly interest and fees;
- qualification estimate;
- amortisation analysis.

The displayed workflow accepts loan amounts from **R500 to R300,000**.

Source: https://www.standardbank.co.za/southafrica/personal/products-and-services/borrow-for-your-needs/personal-loans/loan-calculator

## Savings and investment education

Topics include:
- saving vs investing;
- savings goals;
- emergency funds;
- investment time horizons;
- risk tolerance/appetite;
- tax-free savings;
- unit trusts;
- offshore investing;
- share trading;
- retirement.

Sources:
- https://www.standardbank.co.za/southafrica/personal/learn/savings-and-investments
- https://www.standardbank.co.za/southafrica/personal/learn/a-guide-to-growing-your-money

## Financial planning

Standard Bank's planning content describes a basic process: meet an adviser, discuss current finances and goals, review recommendations and choose implementation. It frames planning as relevant across life stages.

Source: https://www.standardbank.co.za/southafrica/personal/products-and-services/grow-your-money/financial-planning

## Insurance education

Standard Bank publishes explainers on funeral cover, life insurance, travel insurance, credit-card protection, loan protection and how to interpret policy schedules, terms, exclusions, repudiation, extensions and third-party liability.

Sources:
- https://www.standardbank.co.za/southafrica/personal/learn/a-comprehensive-guide-to-insurance-products
- https://www.standardbank.co.za/southafrica/personal/learn/a-simple-guide-to-understanding-your-insurance-contract

## Glossary

The Standard Bank financial glossary explains terms including revolving credit, unsecured loan, unit trust, tax-free savings account, variable rate, virtual card, volatility, risk tolerance, structured products, SWIFT, shortfall and retirement products.

Source: https://www.standardbank.co.za/southafrica/personal/learn/glossary-of-financial-terminology

## Education-source rule

Educational pages are appropriate for explanations and general literacy. They are not substitutes for signed agreements, current pricing guides, product-specific T&Cs, personalised advice or credit approval decisions.
