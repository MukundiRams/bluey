## Limits and payments

### How do I amend my EAP limit?

Your **EAP limit** controls how much money you can withdraw from an account through electronic channels.

To change the limit:

1. Sign in to **Internet Banking**.
2. Select **Transact**.
3. Under **Manage**, select **Limits and card settings**.
4. Select the limit you want to change.
5. Select **Change limit** and follow the instructions.

### How do I make an immediate payment?

Regular EFTs can take several business days to process. Standard Bank allows an immediate payment option through Internet Banking.

To make an immediate payment:

1. Sign in to **Internet Banking**.
2. Select **Transact**.
3. Under **Pay**, select **Beneficiary**.
4. Choose the beneficiary and select **Pay**.
5. When entering the payment details, select **Yes** for **Make an immediate payment?**
6. Complete the payment.

**Important:** An immediate-payment fee applies and is based on the payment amount.

## Statements and account documents

### How do I get a bank statement?

You can view or download statements through Internet Banking.

1. Sign in to **Internet Banking**.
2. Select **Transact**.
3. Under **History & Documents**, select **Statements**.
4. Select the relevant account.
5. Select the statement date.
6. Choose **Download** or **View**.

The FAQ page states that statements are available online for up to **24 months back**.

You can also:
- Get a statement at a Standard Bank ATM (AutoPlus) using your card and PIN.
- Subscribe to receive statements by post or email.

For queries relating to debits, credits or account fees, Standard Bank lists **0860 123 000** as the contact number.

### How do I get my Standard Bank account information without going into a branch?

Standard Bank says you can obtain several documents and manage beneficiaries through the Banking App or Internet Banking.

#### Account confirmation letter

1. Sign in to your account.
2. Select the bank account for which you need confirmation.
3. Scroll to **Your documents**.
4. Select **View**.
5. Scroll to **Bank letters**.
6. Select the three dots next to **Bank account confirmation letter**.
7. Choose to download the letter or send it by email.

#### Settlement quote

1. Sign in to your account.
2. Select the relevant account.
3. Select **Settlement quote**.
4. Submit the request.

The settlement quote will be emailed to you.

#### Manage beneficiaries

1. Sign in to your account.
2. Select **Manage**.
3. Select **Beneficiaries**.
4. View your loaded beneficiaries.
5. Add a new beneficiary or select an existing beneficiary to edit its details.

## Debit orders

### What do I do if I have a debit order dispute?

If the debit order occurred **less than 60 days ago**, Standard Bank says you can dispute it through the **Banking App** or **Online Banking**.

If the debit order occurred **more than 60 days ago**:

1. Call the contact centre on **0860 123 000** to obtain the required mandate form.
2. Alternatively, visit your nearest Standard Bank branch.

## How Standard Bank Mobile works

### How do the Flexi and Top Up plans work?

**Flexi** is a postpaid option that allows you to use your SIM now and pay later for your usage.

**Top Up** is a prepaid option that allows you to buy more airtime once the included airtime value has been used.

### Can I buy data bundles?

Yes. Data bundles can be purchased from the **Manage Area** at `mobile.standardbank.co.za`.

Standard Bank Mobile also allows customers who do not want to be limited to fixed data bundles to change their voice or data rate through voice and data plans for a monthly fee.

### What is a SIM spend limit?

The SIM spend limit allows you to control your monthly spend. It applies to the **Flexi** plan and can be managed by signing in at `www.mobile.standardbank.co.za`.

Spend limits **do not apply while roaming internationally**.

## SIMs, airtime and data

### How do I get a SIM?

Standard Bank Mobile provides SIMs through its Mobile service and associated sales channels. See the current Standard Bank Mobile website for availability and ordering options.

### How do I top up?

You can recharge a Standard Bank Mobile SIM:

- On the Standard Bank Mobile website.
- Through the **Banking App**.
- Through Cellphone Banking by dialling `*120*2345#`.
- At a Standard Bank ATM.

**Flexi note:** For Flexi packages, a recharge first replenishes the used portion of your spend limit; the remaining amount is then allocated to your airtime balance.

### How do I check my balance?

You can:

- Dial `*140#` from your Standard Bank Mobile SIM; or
- Log in at `mobile.standardbank.co.za`.

### Can I transfer my data bundles before they expire?

Yes. Purchased data bundles that are due to expire can be transferred to another Standard Bank Mobile number.

- Transferred data is valid for **7 days**.
- Transfers can be made from the Manage Area or by dialling `*140#`.
- **Rewarded data cannot be transferred.**

### How do I opt in to Out-of-Bundle data services?

You can opt in through the SIM dashboard on the website or by dialling `*140#` and selecting the **Out of Bundle Services** option.

### How do I get billed?

Standard Bank Mobile says you are not debited in the first month following SIM activation. The first payment occurs in the second month after activation.

For example, if the SIM is activated on 2 January, the first debit occurs in February on the debit-order date. That payment may include:

- Pro-rated subscription fees for January.
- February subscription fees.
- Once-off delivery fees.
- Usage charges for January where applicable to Flexi.

## International roaming

### How does International Roaming work?

International Roaming is available to **Flexi customers only**.

To activate it, sign in to your profile and activate international roaming. The FAQ states that **R1,000** must be paid to enable international roaming on the SIM.

After the international network operator's final bill is received, Standard Bank Mobile reconciles the account. Any remaining funds are offset against future invoices until the surplus is used.

Standard Bank Mobile recommends using **international SMS roaming** instead of full international roaming because full roaming can be expensive.

Important points:

- You can be charged for receiving calls while roaming.
- International network operators may submit charges after your return, sometimes up to **90 days later**.
- Published roaming rates are indicative and may change because of exchange-rate movements and international network-operator pricing.
- Your normal SIM spend limit does **not** apply while roaming internationally.

## Porting

### How long does it take to port a number?

The FAQ states that number porting takes approximately **24 to 48 hours** once the porting process has been initiated.

## SIM problems and account locks

### What do I do if my SIM is lost, stolen or damaged?

Sign in to your profile at `www.mobile.standardbank.co.za` and select **Replace my SIM (SIM Swap)**, or call **0801 212 577**.

The current SIM will be soft-locked and a replacement SIM will be sent. You will receive an SMS confirming the SIM-swap request.

The FAQ states that a **SIM replacement fee of R80** applies.

### Why is my SIM account locked and why can't I make calls?

If Standard Bank Mobile does not receive your debit-order payment, the account can be locked. While locked, you cannot:

- Make calls.
- Browse the internet.
- Send SMS messages.

### How do I get my number working again?

If your account is soft-locked:

1. Pay by EFT using **Standard Bank Mobile** as the beneficiary in your banking profile.
2. Allow approximately **3–5 working days** for the payment to clear and reflect in Standard Bank Mobile's account.
3. Once payment reflects, call Customer Care on **0801 212 577** to have the SIM unlocked.

The account is only unlocked after the payment reflects in Standard Bank Mobile's account.

## 24-month contracts

### What happens if my debit order bounces?

The FAQ states:

- **First failed debit order:** You will not receive your monthly airtime or data allocations.
- **Second failed debit order:** You will receive a reminder from debt collectors. The FAQ states that this may result in reporting to the credit bureau and blacklisting.

### How do I arrange payment after a bounced debit order?

Call the Standard Bank Mobile call centre on **0801 212 577**. An agent can assist with payment arrangements.

### How do I get my device removed from the blacklist?

Call **0801 212 577**. A sales agent can assist with payment arrangements and reactivating the device.

### Can I cancel my SIM while I am still paying off the device by debit order?

No. The FAQ states that the device must be paid off before cancelling the SIM contract.

### What happens to the debit order after I return my device?

Once the device return has been approved, the billing team will cancel the device debit order from your bank account.

## Fixed LTE

### How long does Fixed LTE delivery take?

The FAQ states that delivery takes approximately **1–7 business days**.

### Which devices can I use with Standard Bank Fixed LTE?

The FAQ lists these MTN-approved devices:

- Huawei B525
- Huawei B612
- Huawei B618
- ZTE MF286

### Can I use Fixed LTE anywhere?

No. Standard Bank Fixed LTE is designed for a specific home, office or other registered location. The service is locked to the address specified when the order was placed.

### What happens if I move house or office?

You can request to move the Fixed LTE service to another location up to **twice per calendar year**.

Contact Customer Care:

- `0801 212 577`
- `031 819 5321`
- Or dial `135` for free from a Standard Bank Mobile SIM.

### What if I move somewhere without LTE coverage?

Fixed LTE can only be used where coverage is available. If you move to an area without coverage, contact Customer Care to arrange cancellation:

- `0801 212 577`
- `031 819 5321`
- Or dial `135` free from a Standard Bank Mobile SIM.

### Can I have multiple MTN Fixed LTE services?

Yes, provided each service is used at the address for which its application was submitted.

Only one SIM qualifies for a Standard Bank Fixed LTE package based on account type. Additional SIMs can load monthly packages.

### Can I top up or recharge Fixed LTE?

Yes. Sign in at `mobile.standardbank.co.za`, open **Fixed LTE** in the Manage Area and select **Once-off Data Package**.

### Does unused Fixed LTE data roll over?

- Monthly in-bundle data **does not roll over** and is forfeited at the end of the calendar month if unused.
- Top-up data rolls over for an additional **30 days**, making the total validity **60 days from purchase**.

### How do I view my Fixed LTE usage?

Sign in at `mobile.standardbank.co.za` and select **Fixed LTE**.

### Can I cancel my month-to-month Fixed LTE package?

Yes. Contact Customer Care:

- `0801 212 577`
- `031 819 5321`
- Or dial `135` free from a Standard Bank Mobile SIM.

### What if my Fixed LTE SIM is lost, stolen or faulty?

Contact Customer Care to order a replacement SIM and request transfer of any remaining data:

- `0801 212 577`
- `031 819 5321`
- Or dial `135` free from a Standard Bank Mobile SIM.

### My Fixed LTE SIM has been barred. What should I do?

Contact Customer Care on:

- `0801 212 577`
- `031 819 5321`
- Or dial `135` free from a Standard Bank Mobile SIM.

A support agent will assist with the issue.

### Does Fixed LTE provide a private or public IP address?

The FAQ states that Standard Bank Fixed LTE provides a **public dynamic IP address**.

### Is Fixed LTE prioritised on the network tower?

The Fixed LTE coverage map is generated based on capacity and network availability. Standard Bank Mobile states that Fixed LTE customers will not be impacted by congestion issues in the same way as mobile LTE subscribers within the coverage area.

Using an approved MTN router ensures the appropriate firmware configuration for the service.

### What LTE speeds can I expect?

Actual LTE speed depends on several factors, including the device and wireless network conditions.

The FAQ says approved MTN routers can provide speeds from approximately **1 Mbps to 150 Mbps**, but a consistent speed is not guaranteed and the service does not provide an SLA for speed.

### How do I change APN settings on a Fixed LTE router?

The FAQ provides the following general procedure:

1. Insert the MTN LTE SIM into an approved MTN router, ensuring the SIM is correctly sized and positioned.
2. Switch on the router and wait approximately 10 minutes before restarting it to begin provisioning.
3. Connect a laptop or PC through LAN or Wi-Fi. The Wi-Fi name and password should be on the router's underside.
4. Open a web browser and enter the router's management IP address. The FAQ lists:
   - `192.168.8.1`
   - `192.168.0.1`
   The actual address should be confirmed from the router label.
5. Open **Settings** and sign in using the router credentials supplied with the device.
6. Select **Dial Up** and then **Profile Management**.
7. Create a new profile with:
   - **Profile name:** Any name you choose.
   - **Username:** Leave unchanged.
   - **Password:** Leave unchanged.
   - **APN:** `ws.mvnx.fwa`
8. Select the new profile and click **Apply**.
9. Restart the router.
10. Connect to the internet. If necessary, return to the router management page and select **Connect**.
11. If the connection fails, repeat the restart/connect steps periodically over the following 24 hours.
12. If the SIM is still not connected after 24 hours, contact Customer Care on `0801 212 577`, `031 819 5321`, or dial `135` free from a Standard Bank Mobile SIM.

## Product returns and exchanges

### What is the Standard Bank Mobile return policy for unwanted products?

For an unwanted product:

- The product must be in its **original packaging**.
- A return must be requested within **7 days of receipt**.
- Return costs are borne by the customer according to the applicable fee structure.
- The FAQ states that returns are not accepted after 7 days.

### What if my product arrived damaged?

For a product damaged during delivery, call **0801 212 577**, Monday to Friday from **09:00 to 17:30**, or email **support@mobile.standardbank.co.za** to report the damaged shipment.

### What happens if I return a defective product without all accessories?

If accessories or parts originally supplied with the product are missing, Standard Bank Mobile reserves the right, subject to applicable law, to refuse the return, replace the returned item, or estimate the value of the missing components and refund/credit only the applicable amount.

Refunds are processed using the **original payment method**.
