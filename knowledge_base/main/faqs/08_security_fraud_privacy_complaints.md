# Standard Bank South Africa — Security, Fraud, Privacy & Complaints

**Snapshot:** 14 September 2026

## Security Centre

Standard Bank structures security guidance around awareness, protection, prevention and reporting.

Source: https://www.standardbank.co.za/southafrica/personal/products-and-services/security-centre

## Fraud line

- South Africa: **0800 222 050**
- International: **+27 10 824 2090**

Fraud can also be reported through the Banking App.

## Scam types currently covered

- AI-driven spoofing
- email spoofing
- phishing
- vishing/impersonation
- smishing
- stolen-phone scams
- SIM-swap scams
- twin-SIM scams
- spoofed websites
- fake traffic-fine scams
- malware/malicious-software scams

## Core Standard Bank security guidance

- Never share OTPs, PINs or passwords.
- Never approve a transaction you did not initiate.
- Do not transfer funds to a so-called safe account because someone tells you to.
- Do not click unexpected links or scan unknown QR codes.
- Verify suspicious calls independently.
- Report suspicious messages using the channels published by Standard Bank.

Source: https://www.standardbank.co.za/southafrica/personal/products-and-services/security-centre/bank-safely/scams

## SIM-swap indicators

Standard Bank highlights sudden loss of network service, no longer receiving calls/messages, and missing requested OTPs as warning signs.

## Complaints process

### Step 1
Raise the complaint through a branch/manager or the Complaints Resolution Centre at **0860 101 101**. Standard Bank says a reference number and case-manager details are provided and progress is communicated.

### Step 2
Unresolved matters can be referred to the Specialised Complaint Escalation Centre.

### Step 3
If still unresolved, the customer may refer the matter to the applicable ombudsman/regulator under that body's requirements and timeframes.

Source: https://www.standardbank.co.za/southafrica/personal/about-us/regulatory/complaints-process
