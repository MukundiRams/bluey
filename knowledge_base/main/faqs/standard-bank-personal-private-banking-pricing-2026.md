# 2026 pricing overview

Standard Bank states that its **Personal and Private Banking pricing was updated from 1 January 2026**.

The bank describes the 2026 changes as an effort to simplify fee structures and provide more competitive pricing across personal banking products.

The official pricing page provides a searchable collection of **2026 personal and private banking pricing guides**, with the guides being the authoritative place to check detailed account-specific fees.

# Immediate payments

## How much does an immediate payment cost?

Standard Bank's 2026 pricing page lists the following fees:

| Payment value | Immediate-payment fee |
|---|---:|
| Below R100 | **R2** |
| R100 – R1,999.99 | **R7** |
| R2,000 and above | **R50** |

These charges apply to the immediate-payment service; the full account-specific pricing guide should be checked for any additional conditions.

## Why is the fee different depending on the amount?

The 2026 pricing structure uses three payment-value bands:

1. Below R100
2. R100 to R1,999.99
3. R2,000 and above

The fee therefore depends on the value of the immediate payment.

# MyUpdates notifications

## Are MyUpdates notifications free?

Yes, Standard Bank states that notifications are **free for one email address** and are also free when delivered through the **Banking App**.

## How much do additional email notifications cost?

Additional email addresses cost:

**R15 per month**

## How much do SMS notifications cost?

SMS notifications are charged at:

**45 cents per SMS**

Standard Bank states that the total SMS charge is posted to the account at month-end.

## Which notification option is cheapest?

Based on the pricing shown on the 2026 pricing page:

1. **In-app notifications:** Free
2. **One email address:** Free
3. **Additional email addresses:** R15/month each
4. **SMS:** 45c per SMS

# Cash deposits

## How much does it cost to deposit cash at a Standard Bank ATM?

The 2026 pricing page states:

**R1.80 per R100 or part thereof**

This is the highlighted ATM cash-deposit fee.

## Is the fee calculated on the exact deposit amount?

The fee is calculated per **R100 or part thereof**, meaning that a partial R100 amount is treated as a full R100 increment for the purpose of the stated fee.

For example, under the stated formula:

- R100 → R1.80
- R200 → R3.60
- R250 → charged on three R100-or-part-thereof units

The complete account-specific pricing guide should be checked for account-specific exclusions or additional cash-deposit charges.

# Important pricing rules

## When did the 2026 fees become effective?

The pricing changes are effective from:

**1 January 2026**

## Are the published fees inclusive of VAT?

Standard Bank's 2026 pricing guides state that the fees are **inclusive of VAT at 15%**.

## Do daily and monthly thresholds reset on calendar days/months?

Standard Bank's pricing guides state that **daily and monthly fees and thresholds apply on a business-day and business-week cycle**.

Transactions performed after business hours or on public holidays have their fees processed on the next business day, and the thresholds applicable to that next business day apply.

## Can Standard Bank change its pricing?

Yes.

Standard Bank states that its products, services and the terms under which they are offered may change. Customers are advised to read their contracts and consult the latest pricing guides.

## Where should I check the latest pricing?

Standard Bank specifically advises customers to consult the **Pricing** section of its website for the most recent updates.

# Pricing FAQ

## Where can I find Standard Bank's full personal banking pricing?

Use Standard Bank's official Personal and Private Banking pricing page:

https://www.standardbank.co.za/southafrica/personal/about-us/pricing/personal-banking-pricing

The page contains the current pricing guides and allows them to be searched and filtered by year.

## Does the pricing page contain every possible bank fee?

No.

The main page highlights major pricing updates. Detailed fees are contained in the individual pricing guides for the relevant account or product.

## Which fee should I use if two Standard Bank pages show different amounts?

Use the **latest applicable pricing guide for your specific account/product**, and verify its effective date.

Do not assume that a fee for one account applies to another account.

## Are digital transactions generally cheaper?

Standard Bank's pricing structure generally distinguishes between digital/self-service channels and branch transactions. Many account guides provide separate pricing for:

- Banking App / Internet Banking
- ATMs
- Branch transactions

The exact difference depends on the account and transaction type.

## Is cash still charged differently from digital transactions?

Yes. Cash deposits and withdrawals have their own fee structures, and these vary by account.

For example, the 2026 pricing page specifically highlights an ATM cash-deposit fee of R1.80 per R100 or part thereof.

## Are premium-account customers exempt from prepaid top-up fees?

For the products specifically listed by Standard Bank — MyMoPlus, Elite/ACHIEVA, Prestige, Private, Professional and Signature — prepaid airtime, data and electricity top-up fees are stated as free.
